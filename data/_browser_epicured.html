What is Low FODMAP?

The Low FODMAP diet has been clinically proven to allieviate the symptoms of common digestive diseases, including IBS, IBD, and SIBO.

Good news, the Epicured menu includes 100% dietitian-approved low FODMAP meals.

Epicured delivers a diverse medically-tailored menu, curated by clinicians to simplify your Low FODMAP journey.

About Epicured

Epicured is a healthcare company committed to solving chronic disease through nutrition. We believe in using food as medicine to help you feel your best.

Epicured offers an expansive selection of medically tailored food, with over 150 unique items. Our current menu is designed to address digestive health, including IBS, IBD, Crohn's, Colitis and SIBO.

All of our made-to-order meals are chef-prepared and evidence-based. Each item is backed by science in collaboration with our Partners In Health Community.
